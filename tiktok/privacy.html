MeGo Reels – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

MeGo Reels is a private tool used only by MeGo (https://megoo.me, Instagram/TikTok @_megoo.me) to publish its own short educational videos to its own TikTok account.

What we access

When the MeGo account owner connects TikTok, the tool receives an access token and basic account info (open ID, display name) for that one account, with permission to upload and publish videos. It does not access, collect or store data about any other TikTok users, viewers or followers.

How it is used

The token is used only to upload MeGo's own pre-made videos and captions. Tokens are stored as encrypted GitHub Actions secrets and are never shared or sold.

Removal

Access can be revoked at any time from TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions. Contact: through https://megoo.me.
