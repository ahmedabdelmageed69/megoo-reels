# Daily Reel style rules (owner's standing requests)
- Colors: tools/reel_anim.py rotates a soft palette by date automatically; never hardcode a palette in post.json.
- Music: tools/hum.py is a soft, quiet pad that changes key/progression daily (seeded by date). Keep it gentle, never loud.
- Content must feel emotional and real, not just information: open with a relatable pain/moment (a lost photo, a stressful bill, a late-night worry), write each point as a tiny scene (❌ زمان feeling → ✅ دلوقتي relief), speak to "you", cover hook asks a question or names the pain, CTA invites sending it to a specific person. Still exact apps and steps, no invented facts.
